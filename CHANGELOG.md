# Changelog

## 1.4.0
- New month grid layout: multi-day bars and single-day entries fill free rows per day (no more empty gaps, e.g. when a bar only covers Fri–Sun); nothing spills over into the next week; hidden entries are counted as “↓ n” in the day header.
- Neues Rasterlayout: Balken und Tageseinträge füllen freie Zeilen je Tag (keine Leerplätze mehr); nichts ragt in die nächste Woche; nicht sichtbare Einträge zeigt „↓ n“ im Tageskopf.
- Holidays are shown in the day header instead of the weekday name / Feiertage stehen im Tageskopf statt des Wochentags.
- New holiday regions: Switzerland, France, Italy, Spain (1900–2100, historical rules, e.g. Italy's San Francesco 4 Oct 1958–1976 and from 2026, Festa della Repubblica on the first Sunday of June 1977–2000, France's Victoire 1945, Spain's Día de la Constitución from 1983); cantonal/regional holidays as special days.
- Neue Regionen: Schweiz, Frankreich, Italien, Spanien; Auswahl in der Seitenleiste zweispaltig.

## 1.3.0
- Holidays and special days 1900–2100, selectable per radio button: none, Austria, Germany, England & Wales, USA; special days can be switched on/off.
- Feiertage und Sondertage 1900–2100, per Radiobutton wählbar: keine, Österreich, Deutschland, England & Wales, USA; Sondertage ein-/ausschaltbar.
- Austria: holidays and special days taken from Endless Calendar Pro (Opernball, Fasching, Easter days, Mother's/Father's Day, Advent, clock change, Martini, Christmas Eve …); Nationalfeiertag from 1965, clock change from 1980.
- Germany: nationwide holidays (Tag der Deutschen Einheit 17 June 1954–1989, 3 October from 1990, Reformationstag 2017); regional holidays and customs as special days.
- England & Wales: bank holidays with substitute days, historical rules (Whit Monday/August bank holiday before 1971) and one-off holidays (jubilees, royal weddings, 2022 state funeral, 2023 coronation).
- USA: federal holidays with observed days and historical dates (e.g. Memorial Day 30 May before 1971, Juneteenth from 2021).
- Holidays colour the day header like a weekend and appear in the grid, the mini calendar, the week list and the day list.

## 1.2.2 – first public version / erste öffentliche Version
- Clear month view in the style of classic desktop planners, in its own tab: six-week grid, week numbers, coloured day headers, one-line entries, multi-day events as bars.
- Flip month by month with the mouse wheel, PgUp/PgDn, Home = today; neighbouring months are preloaded.
- Double-click to open or create events (Thunderbird's own dialog), right-click menu for hide, compact, export, delete (with backup).
- Sidebar with mini calendar and the next seven days; pastel colours; overflow indicator with day list.
- Compact entries stay readable; status bar shows “all series compact”.
- German and English user interface, MIT licence.
